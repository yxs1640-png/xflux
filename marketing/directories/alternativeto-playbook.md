## 5. Do / Don’t

**Do**

- One account, factual descriptions, real screenshots
- Add XFlux as alternative on 5+ competitor pages over a week (not all in one hour)
- Reply if moderators ask for clarification

**Don’t**

- Spam the same text on 20 pages in one session
- Create duplicate XFlux listings
- Claim “official Twitter partner” or “real-time streaming”
